# Farm Batches alpha admission

Farm Batches is a company-scoped poultry workflow. A company administrator enables the farm_operations vertical pack in Company Setup. The workspace and built-in Assistant actions additionally require the corresponding farm_batch permission. The pack does not admit the Manufacturing workspace.

Use the batch workflow for dated intake, weigh-ins, feed, mortality, transfers, processing records, and links to verified ERP records. CSV and values-only XLSX use the saved import task and the same farm event owner as manual entry. Historic observations preserve the original business date. Existing inventory and accounting entries can be linked where their exact identities are known.

Processing uses a compatible Manufacturing order and BOM, with exact live and output lots. Retained orders and movements support checking or resuming an interrupted run. The financial view distinguishes verified linked posted costs from complete cost per sellable kilogram and margin. Those metrics require reconciled production costs and a native allocation from posted invoice and credit lines to the exact delivery and return lots.

Keep a pilot company disabled until the target release has reconciled one flock from import through its native batch, stock, and accounting records. Verify replay after interruptions and compare as-of headcount and metrics to independently checked totals. Enter and read back events through the workspace and the built-in Assistant.
